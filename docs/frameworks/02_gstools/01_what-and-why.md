# GSTools, what it is and why this one

## What it is

Geostatistical field generation and variogram modelling. Sequential Gaussian
simulation over Gaussian, exponential, Matern and stable models. BSD-3-Clause. Muller, Schuler, Zech
and Hesse, Geosci. Model Dev. 15, 3161-3182 (2022), doi:10.5194/gmd-15-3161-2022.

## Why this and not something else

The live lane already generates a correlated stream from an exact exponential
recursion, so this is not here for correlation as such. It is here to break a circularity.

Two of the product's claims depend on the input variogram: that layers stop being independent once
the correlation range exceeds one layer's tonnage, and that the app can RECOVER the input structure
from the reclaimed stream. Testing a recovery routine against a stream generated by the same closed
form it assumes proves nothing. GSTools generates the field by its own spectral method, over model
families the recovery does not assume, and the fit is then scored against a range and sill it was
never told.
